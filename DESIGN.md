## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the durability, utility, and straightforward reliability of a commercial-grade trade counter. It is engineered for professional contractors, tradespeople, mechanics, and serious DIYers who prioritize stock clarity, load specifications, and transparent pricing over decorative flair.

The design movement combines **Industrial Utilitarianism** with **Modern Tactile Precision**:
- **Utilitarian Sturdiness:** Elements communicate structural permanence through thick strokes, solid surfaces, and deliberate architectural layouts that resemble blueprint grids and warehouse bin markers.
- **Physical Clarity:** High-contrast indicator badges ('STOCK DISPONIBLE', 'BULTO CERRADO', 'OFERTA GREMIO') mirror stamped metal nameplates and safety signage.
- **Zero Tech Fluff:** No diffuse colored glows, generic soft gradients, or translucent glass. Every card, container, and button feels fabricated, physical, and instantly readable under harsh warehouse lights or bright outdoor glare.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from heavy machinery, structural steel, and worksite safety warnings:

- **Graphite & Charcoal Base (`#1E232A`, `#121519`):** Primary structural surface for headers, heavy-duty footers, key actions, and dominant tool typography. Provides weight and authoritative anchor points.
- **Industrial Safety Amber (`#F59E0B`, deep shade `#D97706`):** The functional highlight tone. Used strictly for critical alerts, high-conversion purchase triggers, promotional banners ('OFERTA'), and technical rating accents.
- **Construction Orange (`#EA580C`):** Reserved for urgent badges, clearance pricing, and inventory thresholds (e.g., "ÚLTIMAS 3 UNIDADES").
- **Off-White Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`):** High-clarity background tone that minimizes eye fatigue while keeping SKU imagery and tabular specs crisp.
- **Structural Wire Borders (`#CBD5E1`, `#334155`):** Explicit dividing lines that frame content blocks, imitating structural metal joinery.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scanning in high-friction environments:

- **Display & Headlines (Chivo):** An aggressive, geometric grotesque with tight tracking and stout stems that feels machined. Used for category headers, hero value propositions, and SKU titles.
- **Body & Tabular Data (Work Sans):** Clean, open-aperture sans-serif optimized for fine technical copy, dimensional specifications (mm, pulgadas, watts), warranty conditions, and invoice tables.
- **Badges, Tags & Technical Codes (Space Grotesk):** Monoline technical grotesque used uppercase for SKUs, part numbers, warehouse aisle indicators, and inventory statuses.
- **Pricing Notation:** Prices in ARS use oversized `price-display` styles with explicit punctuation and aligned currency signs (`$`) to guarantee quick recognition on small screens and busy sales aisles.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an engineering blueprint discipline:

- **Layout Structure:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`1280px+`) with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (3rem). Tablets (`768px-1279px`) fold to 8 columns with `gutter-md` (1.5rem). Mobile devices (`<768px`) collapse to 4 columns with `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1rem).
- **Component Rhythms:** Compact vertical rhythm designed to minimize excessive scrolling for dense industrial inventories. Related items cluster with `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while major functional zones step back by `space-lg` and `space-xl`.
- **Data Alignment:** Technical parameter sheets, dimensional tables, and cart listings use strict gridlines and boundary borders to facilitate horizontal cross-referencing.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cloud shadows, mimicking solid stamped materials instead:

- **Layer 0 (Baseplate):** Neutral backdrop (`#F8FAFC`) framed by crisp 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Tool Trays):** Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed with an architectural 1.5px solid border (`#CBD5E1`) and a hard-edged technical micro-shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(18, 21, 25, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Trays & Flyouts):** Dark graphite headers or active modals use deep tactile drops: `0 4px 0px #1E232A`, providing a pressed-button mechanical look.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Technical Drawers):** High-contrast graphite backgrounds (`#121519`) layered on a 60% matte slate scrim (`#0F172A`).

## Shapes

The form factor is deliberately geometric and chiseled (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Core Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, SKU cards, and notification boxes. Provides just enough relief to avoid sharp razor-corners while preserving industrial rigidity.
- **Container Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px for large modules):** Applied strictly to overarching modal dialogues and section frames.
- **Zero-Pill Doctrine:** Round pill shapes are strictly prohibited. Badges, tags, and buttons must retain crisp rectangular contours reminiscent of sheet metal panels and stencil tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial Button:** Solid `#1E232A` graphite background, `#FFFFFF` uppercase label in Space Grotesk 14px bold, with a bottom 2px offset border in `#121519`. Active state produces an authentic mechanical press (`translate-y-0.5`).
- **Caution / Action Button (Checkout & Urgent Buy):** `#F59E0B` background with `#121519` high-contrast bold typography. Hover darkens to `#D97706`.
- **Secondary (Spec Sheet / Bulk Quote):** Transparent fill with a 1.5px border in `#1E232A` and `#1E232A` text.

### Product Cards
- Contained within a 1.5px `#E2E8F0` border that transitions to `#1E232A` on hover.
- High-contrast visual header allocating dedicated space for technical badges (`OFERTA`, `STOCK INMEDIATO`).
- Large price presentation in ARS (`$XX.XXX,XX`) paired with subtle VAT/IVA distinction (`IVA Inc.`).
- Prominent secondary line indicating packaging type ("Venta por bulto: 50 u." or "Unidad").

### Chips & Badges
- **'STOCK DISPONIBLE':** `#ECFDF5` background with bold `#065F46` border and text. Accompanied by a solid green square beacon icon.
- **'SIN STOCK / A PEDIDO':** `#F1F5F9` background, `#64748B` slate border and copy.
- **'OFERTA':** Solid `#F59E0B` amber background with `#1E232A` bold black stencil lettering.
- **'PRECIO GREMIO':** Solid `#EA580C` orange badge with white lettering for commercial discounts.

### Form Inputs & Quantities
- Inputs framed with 1.5px `#94A3B8` borders, flat white backgrounds, and deep `#0F172A` text.
- Focus state applies a thick 2px `#1E232A` ring with zero soft glow.
- Numeric quantity steppers feature heavy plus/minus blocks designed for swift glove-friendly tapping on job sites.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes with 2px solid `#334155` borders. Selected states fill completely with `#1E232A` housing an angular white checkmark.

### Technical Spec Tables
- Alternating row zebra fills (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with `#E2E8F0` horizontal hairline dividers. Key specs (potencia, torque, rosca, material) highlighted in semi-bold Space Grotesk.
